SnapOCR Privacy Policy

SnapOCR does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Screenshots and recognised text are processed entirely on your device. The extension makes no network requests at all unless you use a non-bundled language or activate a Pro licence.

01Data collection

SnapOCR collects no personal information. Specifically:

  • No account, email address or name is requested.
  • No screenshots, recognised text or clipboard contents are transmitted.
  • No browsing history, tab URLs, cookies or form data are read or stored.
  • No analytics, crash reports or telemetry are sent.
The screenshot you select exists only in the browser's memory, is cropped locally, and is discarded when the result window closes.

02How SnapOCR works

The OCR engine (Tesseract.js, compiled to WebAssembly) and the language data for English and Simplified Chinese are bundled with the extension. When you drag a selection, the visible tab is captured with the browser's own screenshot API, cropped on your device with a canvas, and passed to the local engine. Nothing is uploaded at any step.

03Permissions

  • activeTab — reads the current tab only at the moment you start a capture; there is no standing access to sites you have not acted on.
  • scripting — injects the selection overlay into the current tab when you invoke a capture.
  • contextMenus — adds the right-click entry.
  • storage / unlimitedStorage — saves settings, history and cached language packs on your device.
  • clipboardWrite — copies recognised text when you click Copy.
  • Host permission api.annmax1983.com — used only for optional Pro licence activation and validation.

04Network requests

There are exactly two situations in which SnapOCR makes an outbound request, and neither includes page content or screenshots:

  • Language packs. If you choose one of the seven non-bundled languages, its model file is downloaded once from a public CDN and then cached on your device.
  • Licence check. Only if you activate a Pro licence. A random installation identifier and basic browser metadata are sent, approximately every two hours while the extension is in use.

If you never activate a licence and stay with the bundled languages, SnapOCR makes no network requests at all.

05Local storage

The following is stored on your device and never synced to us:

  • Your selected recognition language and interface preferences.
  • Recognition history, capped at 10 entries on the free plan and 200 on Pro.
  • Cached language models, so offline recognition keeps working.
  • Licence state, if you activated one.

Clearing history in the popup or removing the extension deletes this data from your device.

06Licence data

Pro licences are issued by our payment provider and validated through our own licence service. The service receives the licence key you enter, a randomly generated installation identifier, the extension identifier and basic browser metadata (browser name and version, UI language, time zone, user agent). It does not receive screenshots, recognised text or page content. Licence records are kept to enforce activation limits and to handle support requests.

07Third parties

We do not sell, rent or share data with advertisers or data brokers. Two service providers are involved strictly to operate the product: our payment provider (licence purchase) and our hosting provider (licence validation and language-pack delivery). No third-party analytics or advertising scripts are included in the extension.

08Your choices

  • Use SnapOCR entirely offline with the bundled languages, with no licence and no network traffic.
  • Clear recognition history at any time from the popup.
  • Deactivate a licence from Settings to free the activation slot.
  • Uninstall the extension, which removes all locally stored data.
