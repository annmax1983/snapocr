SnapOCR is a screenshot OCR extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Drag a box over any part of a page and the words inside it become editable text you can copy. The OCR engine runs on your device, so screenshots are never uploaded, no account is needed, and it keeps working with the network switched off.

01

Everything it does

A small tool with a narrow job: get the text out, fast, without sending your screen anywhere.

01

Drag a box over any part of a page and the text inside it becomes selectable.

02

Recognition runs on your device with a bundled Tesseract OCR engine — the image is discarded when the result window closes.

03

English and Simplified Chinese ship inside the extension and need no network.

04

Seven more languages (Pro) download once, then work offline too.

05

Start from the right-click menu or the toolbar button; Esc cancels before anything is captured.

06

Results are editable — fix a misread character before copying.

Also included

  • One click copies the text to the clipboard, ready for chat, email or a document.
  • The crop uses your real device pixel ratio, so text stays sharp on HiDPI screens.
  • Recognition history: the last 10 results free, 200 with Pro.
  • TXT export for saved results (Pro).
  • No permanent access to every site — the selection overlay is injected only when you invoke SnapOCR.
  • Six interface languages, no account and no tracking.

02

Free vs Pro

The free version is permanent. Pro adds two things and removes nothing.

Free

20 captures a day (resets at local midnight), offline recognition in English and Simplified Chinese, editable results, one-click copy, history of your last 10 captures and six interface languages. No watermark and no account.

Pro

$14.99 one-off: unlimited captures, seven more languages (Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, French, German, Spanish), 200 history records and TXT export. One licence activates on two browsers, and removing the licence frees that slot again.

The licence is optional. Without one the extension makes no network requests; once active it re-checks with the licence server about every two hours, and a failed check never locks anything.

Private by design

Your screenshots and recognized text never leave the browser. No account, no analytics, no tracking — and no OCR server behind the extension.
